## Codebase Patterns

- Existing mode tokens are `agg` and `seq`; shared base executions are recorded in every mode Ledger.
- Existing budgets are `R=4k` rollouts and `P=3k` improvement stages; final selection and reflection are recorded separately.
- `RunView` never carries a score. `adapter.score` belongs to independent post-selection judging only.
- Complete-task resume, deterministic labels/run ids, Proposer caching, and per-mode Libraries are existing contracts.
- The deterministic Demo executes real Python and requires no model credentials.
